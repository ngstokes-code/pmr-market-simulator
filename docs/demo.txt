1. Sellers and buyers rest. Two sells share the price 100.02.
  > sell #1 5 @ 100.03: accepted, executed 0, rested 5
  > sell #2 3 @ 100.02: accepted, executed 0, rested 3
  > sell #3 4 @ 100.02: accepted, executed 0, rested 4
  > sell #4 10 @ 100.05: accepted, executed 0, rested 10
  > buy  #5 6 @  99.98: accepted, executed 0, rested 6
  > buy  #6 2 @  99.99: accepted, executed 0, rested 2
      ask 100.05 | #4 x10
      ask 100.03 | #1 x5
      ask 100.02 | #2 x3 #3 x4
      ------------ spread 3 ticks
      bid  99.99 | #6 x2
      bid  99.98 | #5 x6

2. A buy for 5 at 100.02. Price first: it takes the lowest ask. Then time: at 100.02, #2 arrived before #3, so #2 executes in full first.
  > buy  #7 5 @ 100.02: accepted, executed 5, rested 0
      execution: #7 x3 @ 100.02 against resting #2
      execution: #7 x2 @ 100.02 against resting #3
      ask 100.05 | #4 x10
      ask 100.03 | #1 x5
      ask 100.02 | #3 x2
      ------------ spread 3 ticks
      bid  99.99 | #6 x2
      bid  99.98 | #5 x6

3. #3 cancels. Its level empties, so the best ask moves up a tick.
  > cancel #3: removed
      ask 100.05 | #4 x10
      ask 100.03 | #1 x5
      ------------ spread 4 ticks
      bid  99.99 | #6 x2
      bid  99.98 | #5 x6
